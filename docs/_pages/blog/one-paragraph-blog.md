Often times I find it difficult to finish writing.  To solve for this I will try to keep my writings short and concise, the [fluff](https://libguides.usc.edu/writingguide/CARS)[\comment, Swales et al. 1990 introduces an interesting way to introduce papers using his CARS models, I will forgo this.  Unfortunately sometimes this may result in confused readers] will be left as links or other posts.  The primary point of these paragraph posts will be to convey the idea and practice finalizing thoughts concisely.
